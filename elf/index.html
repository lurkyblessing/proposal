e.l.f. Speed Runs

High-energy TikTok challenge highlighting accessibility and performance under pressure.

The Concept

Can you do a full face of flawless makeup in under 3 minutes? We challenge our community and top influencers to an "e.l.f. Speed Run." This highlights the blendability, ease of use, and incredible formulas of e.l.f. products. It’s fast, chaotic, highly engaging, and perfectly tailored for the TikTok algorithm.

  • Goal: Viral engagement & showcasing product blendability.
  • Platform: TikTok & Instagram Reels.
  • Format: Split-screen timer vs. the creator.

Dupe Drop Radar

Gamifying affordable beauty like exclusive streetwear sneaker drops.

The Concept

e.l.f. is famous for breaking the internet with high-quality dupes of luxury products. Why not treat these releases like highly anticipated sneaker drops? The "Dupe Drop Radar" is a digital countdown on the app/site. Users sign up for SMS alerts to get early access to limited-edition drops before they sell out.

  • Goal: Build massive hype, collect SMS contacts, and drive instant sell-outs.
  • Mechanism: Countdown clocks, blurred product teasers, and SMS notifications.
  • Vibe: Exclusive, urgent, hypebeast culture meets beauty.
